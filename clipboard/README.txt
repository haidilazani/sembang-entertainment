SEMBANG — RASPBERRY CLIPBOARD

Open "Sembang Studio Props Preview.html" in a browser and choose "Clipboard".
Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, or use the Front / Back / Edge buttons.

MODELS
Sembang_Clipboard.glb — complete model, texture embedded
Sembang_Clipboard.obj + .mtl — editable model (keep Raspberry_Board.png beside them)
Raspberry_Board.png — subtle procedural board grain texture

DESIGN
Modelled from the supplied reference, recoloured in the Sembang raspberry and
cream palette: a raspberry board with a cream clip at the top and a blank
cream sheet of paper.
The board has rounded corners and softly rolled edges with a gently darkened
rim and a subtle mottled surface texture. The paper is a thin rounded
sheet with a second sheet peeking out beneath it. The clip is a bent-plate
shape wrapping over the top edge, with a raised lower jaw, a dark paper slit,
a hanging eyelet ring and rivets on both sides.

COLOURS
Raspberry #EF4C7A (board, lightly textured), cream #FBFACF (paper, clip),
#F1EEBE (under-sheet), blush #F9BFC5 (jaw, ring, rivets), deep raspberry
#961E48 (slit and eyelet opening). Satin, non-metallic. GLB colour factors are
linear RGB.

MODEL DETAILS
Y is up; the paper side faces +Z. 3.6 units wide, 5.3 units tall (about
5.6 with the clip), 0.1 units thick; 0.32 units deep at the clip.
glTF units are metres; scale to the size you need.
12 named, individually closed mesh components that overlap as an assembled object.
The paper (Paper_Sheet) is a separate mesh, ready to remove or swap.
