SEMBANG — PISTOL-GRIP MEGAPHONE

Open "Sembang Studio Props Preview.html" in a browser and choose "Megaphone".
Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, or use the Front / Back / Edge buttons
(Edge looks at the vented rear cap).

MODELS
Sembang_Megaphone.glb — complete model
Sembang_Megaphone.obj + .mtl — editable model (keep them together)

DESIGN
Modelled from the supplied reference: a cream horn flaring into a rolled
raspberry bell, a raspberry rear cap with vent slots, gold trim rings at the
collar and bell, a cream driver tube with a gold button inside the bell, and a
cream-and-raspberry pistol grip with a gold trigger, band and pin.
The horn, bell, cap, tube and trim are turned as smooth solids of revolution
with rounded edges. The Sembang S and star emblem (traced design 01, from
source/01_raspberry_cream.svg) is bent to follow the curve of the horn, with a
cream outline, raspberry S and star, and cream highlight strokes.

COLOURS
Raspberry #EF4C7A, cream #FBFACF, emblem outline #F0E4AC, gold #D6AB5C,
vent slots #961E48. Satin/gloss plastic; gold is semi-metallic.
GLB colour factors are linear RGB.

MODEL DETAILS
Y is up; the bell opens toward +X, the grip hangs down, and the emblem faces +Z.
About 5 units long and 4.9 units tall including the grip; the bell is about
4 units across. glTF units are metres; scale to the size you need.
31 named, individually closed mesh components that overlap as an assembled
object (rendering/animation assets, not a boolean-unioned print mesh).
Emblem_* meshes sit just above the horn surface and can be hidden or recoloured.
